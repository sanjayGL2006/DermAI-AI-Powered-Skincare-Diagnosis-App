Section 01

A-to-Z Application Knowledge & Information

⚡

1.1 Purpose & Mission

DermAI is an advanced AI-powered skincare assessment platform designed and built by Sanjay GL. It combines multimodal artificial intelligence with dermatological principles to analyze facial images, identify skin concerns, and generate customized treatment routines.

⚙️

1.2 Architecture & Tech Stack

  • Backend: Python Flask microservices core (`app.py`), relational database management (`database.py`), Firebase Admin SDK.
  • AI Engine: Multimodal Google Gemini 1.5 Flash vision intelligence model.
  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS design system, Vanilla JS, PWA service worker offline caching (`sw.js`).
  • Auth: Google OAuth 2.0 (`flask_dance`) and secure local session tokens.

1.3 Core Workflows & Features

  1. Smart Questionnaire & Scan: Captures user skin feel (oily, dry, sensitive), acne severity, lifestyle factors, and live photo upload.
  2. AI Condition Detection: Evaluates 10+ skin parameters (Acne, Hyperpigmentation, Dark Circles, Enlarged Pores, Dehydration, Rosacea).
  3. Routine & Product Engine: Generates tailored Morning (AM) and Evening (PM) steps with Indian market price benchmarks (₹ INR) and Nykaa/Amazon links.
  4. Interactive AI Assistant: DermAI chatbot provides context-aware follow-up answers linked to user scan history.
Section 02

Full Detailed Data Privacy Policy

Official Policy Effective Date: August 18, 2026

2.1 Commitment to User Privacy

Your privacy is strictly safeguarded by Sanjay GL. We adhere to India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023, global GDPR data minimization rules, and health data confidentiality standards.

2.2 Facial Image Data & 5-Hour 1-Minute Automatic Purge Policy

  • Zero Permanent Image Storage: Facial photos uploaded for diagnostic analysis are evaluated ephemerally in server volatile memory (RAM). Photos are never saved to disk or permanent databases.
  • 🕒 5-Hour 1-Minute Auto-Purge Guarantee: Any temporary image buffer, uploaded photo asset, or sensitive session trace is automatically deleted after 5 hours 1 minute (18,060 seconds) max by our automated background cleanup protocol.
  • No Facial Biometric Tracking: We do not create biometric templates, face recognition IDs, or identity tracking maps for any person.
  • Instant Buffer Disposal: Immediately after the AI assessment is completed, the image RAM buffer is fully purged.
